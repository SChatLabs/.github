# SChat — Safety Chat

**Advancing secure, censorship-resistant communications.**

SChat is an independent engineering team building privacy-first communication infrastructure. Our mission is to ensure that personal and group digital correspondence remains fundamentally anonymous, encrypted, and user-controlled.

---

## Core Architecture

### 🛡️ SChat Messenger
A decentralized, censorship-resistant messenger engineered for uncompromising privacy and anonymity.
* **Tor-Routed Architecture:** All network traffic is routed natively through the Tor network, obscuring IP addresses, metadata, and physical locations by design.
* **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE):** Cryptographic protection ensures that messages, media, and calls can only be decrypted by the intended participants—never by servers or third parties.
* **Zero-Knowledge Principles:** Built with minimal metadata retention and local-first security controls.
* **Private AI Integration:** Powered by **SCAI** (`SCAI 1.0 Inception`) in partnership with **Prospective** to deliver intelligent dialogue assistance inside an encrypted environment.

---

## Principles

1. **Privacy by Architecture:** Security is not an optional feature; it is enforced at the network and cryptographic layers.
2. **Digital Sovereignty:** Users maintain full control over their keys, identity, and communications.
3. **Resilience:** Built to operate reliably under strict network restrictions and surveillance.
